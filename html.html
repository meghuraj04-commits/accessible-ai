What do these checks mean?

Web accessibility helps people with visual, hearing, motor, and cognitive disabilities use websites. WebForAll aims to identify barriers and provide practical guidance based on WCAG accessibility principles.

Automated tools can detect many common problems, but they cannot confirm every aspect of accessibility. Manual testing is also important.
